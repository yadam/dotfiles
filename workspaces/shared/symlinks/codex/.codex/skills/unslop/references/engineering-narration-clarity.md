# Engineering-video narration clarity

Use this as a light clarity pass during humanizer's editing of engineering
video narration. Humanizer and the supplied voice profile govern cadence,
personality, and the final self-audit. This is not a second style overhaul.

- Explain what the software does and what the viewer observes. Replace
  unsupported praise with a concrete behavior supported by the script's
  evidence. If that evidence is absent, shorten the claim rather than invent
  a mechanism, number, cause, or result.
- Introduce a necessary concept before relying on it. Keep one consistent
  name for it; define unfamiliar technical terms for the intended audience.
- Preserve the reasoning from problem to decision to observable consequence.
  Cut repetition and filler without removing why the decision mattered.
- Tie narration to actual footage, records, or checks. Distinguish planned
  behavior, observed behavior, measured results, and untested possibilities.
  A narrow passing check supports a narrow conclusion.
- Keep genuine uncertainty and the recorded contributions of both the human
  and the assistant. Do not turn a collaboration into an unsupported claim
  that one prompt or one tool completed the work.
- Read aloud. Split a sentence when a listener would lose the thread;
  preserve varied sentence lengths, natural transitions, personal reactions,
  useful analogies, and concrete sensory details. Punctuation and wording
  follow the voice and project guidance, not a blacklist.

Apply only checks that improve this passage. Do not require a stock outline,
new explanation, or invented dramatic beat. Let humanizer's final self-audit
revert edits that flatten the voice or change the meaning.
